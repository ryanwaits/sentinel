Decision — shipping both

01 — Text lockup and 02 — Mark + wordmark both ship, as two variants of one component. Sentinel stays text-only (no new glyph) — only secondlayer's side gets the real mark.

Text is the default: audit card footer, /log footer, anywhere already-quiet copy lives. Mark + wordmark is for first-contact moments — the sign-in panel and top nav — where a new user has no context yet and the real glyph earns a half-second more trust than text alone. One component, a variant="text" | "mark" prop, same underlying tokens. 03–06 stay parked here as reference, not adopted.

  2. Still open: confirm secondlayer is fine with their exact #2563eb appearing on Sentinel's surface. Some infra brands only permit a monochrome/greyscale mark for third-party attribution — if so, the mark + wordmark variant needs a grayscale glyph option.
