# Sentinel — PRODUCT

register: product

## Product purpose
Audit-informed security monitoring for Stacks smart contracts. Sentinel pairs **Prevention** (a multi-agent
audit engine that reproduces exploits in an airgapped sandbox) with **Detection** (continuous, context-aware
monitoring scoped by that audit), in one system, pre-launch through production. Every alert is human-gated;
Sentinel never acts on-chain itself.

## Users
Security-minded smart-contract engineers and protocol security leads on Stacks. They read Clarity and know
what a finding, PoC, timelock, and outflow are. They live in terminals and dashboards (Linear, Datadog,
GitHub). They want signal, not noise: a paged alert must be reproduced or correlated to a proven bug, with
enough context to act in minutes.

## Brand & positioning (framing borrowed from Datadog)
Datadog's move is pairing two capabilities into one platform ("Observability and Security"). Sentinel's two
are **Prevention + Detection**. The moat: monitoring scoped by an actual audit of *your* contracts, not
generic rules. A confirmed audit finding becomes the exact signal monitoring watches for being exploited.

## Voice & tone
Confident, technical, calm, honest. State what it does plainly; no hype, no emoji cheer. Precise domain
terms used correctly. Outcome-first verbs: audit, detect, correlate, reproduce, alert, act. Calm even at
critical severity, a security tool, not a klaxon. Honesty is load-bearing: Type-2 is detection ("already
on-chain"), a signature match is a correlation (not a confirmed exploit), disclosure is human-gated. The
copy's restraint is the credibility. Micro-copy is coverage-forward: an empty state names what's watched,
it doesn't congratulate.

## Anti-references (do not look or sound like)
- Neon-on-black "crypto security" aesthetic (the first category reflex).
- Dark-blue observability-dashboard cliché (the second reflex).
- SaaS hype: "You're all caught up!", exclamation points, "blazing-fast".
- Alarm-red-everything klaxon UX; fear-selling.
- Generic identical-card dashboards and hero-metric templates.

## Strategic principles
- Signal over noise: a cheap pre-filter drops benign events; only notable ones surface.
- Every claim earns trust: bug vs centralization labeled honestly, PoC-backed where possible.
- Human-gated always: Sentinel routes intent; a human acts.
- Prevention and Detection are distinct motions; the UI must make which one you're looking at obvious.
